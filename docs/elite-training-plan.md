# Elite Training Plan

## Pace Key

- **E** — Easy pace
- **M** — Marathon pace
- **T** — Threshold pace
- **I** — Interval pace
- **R** — Repetition pace

## Phase A

### Weeks 1–3 (24–22 weeks before race)

- Run 7 days per week for at least 30 minutes at E pace.
- Add more than 30 minutes only after establishing this base.

### Weeks 4–6 (21–19 weeks before race)

- 1 long run: 25% of the week's total mileage or 2.5 hours, whichever is less.
- 6 days of 30 minutes or more at E pace.
- After 2 or 3 E runs, add 6–8 × 20–30-second strides.

### Week 24 — Race week

- **7 days to race (Q1):** 90 minutes E
- **6 days to race:** 60 minutes E + 4–6 strides
- **5 days to race (Q2):** 2 miles E + 4 × 1,200 m T with 2 minutes E + 2 miles E
- **4 days to race:** 30–50 minutes E + 4–6 strides
- **3 days to race:** 20–30 minutes E + 4 strides
- **2 days to race:** 0–20 minutes E
- **1 day to race:** 20–30 minutes E

## VDOT Note

At 12 and 6 weeks before the race, consider increasing VDOT by one unit—or as dictated by a race result.
