# Heart-Rate Zone Chart

| Zone | Primary purpose | Approx. %HRR | %HRmax |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **E — Easy** | Aerobic development and recovery | 60–74% | 65–79% |
| **M — Marathon pace** | Marathon-specific aerobic work | 75–84% | 80–90% |
| **T — Threshold** | Lactate-threshold endurance | 83–88% | 88–92% |
| **I — Intervals** | VO₂max development | 95–100% | 98–100% |
| **R — Repetitions** | Speed and running economy | Not practical | Not practical |

## HRR Formula

$$
HR_{target} = HR_{rest} + \left(\text{intensity} \times (HR_{max} - HR_{rest})\right)
$$

Where intensity is expressed as a decimal. For example, with a resting heart rate of 50 bpm and maximum heart rate of 190 bpm, a 70% HRR target is:

$$
50 + \left(0.70 \times (190 - 50)\right) = 148\text{ bpm}
$$

> The %HRR ranges are practical approximations. The source table directly specifies %VO₂max and %HRmax, not %HRR.
