# Types of Training: Purpose, Intensity, and Duration

| Zone | Purpose | Intensity | Variety | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E — Easy** | Promote desirable cell changes and develop the cardiovascular system | 59–74% VO₂max; 65–79% HRmax | Warm-up | 10–30 min |
| **E — Easy** | Same as above | Same as above | Cool-down | 10–30 min |
| **E — Easy** | Same as above | Same as above | Recovery run | 30–60 min |
| **E — Easy** | Same as above | Same as above | Recovery within a workout | Up to several minutes |
| **E — Easy** | Same as above | Same as above | Long run | Up to the lesser of 150 min and 25% of the week's total mileage |
| **M — Marathon pace** | Experience marathon race-pace conditions; alternative easy pace for others | 75–84% VO₂max; 80–90% HRmax | Steady run or long repeats | Up to the lesser of 90 min and 16 miles |
| **T — Threshold** | Improve endurance | 83–88% VO₂max; 88–92% HRmax | Tempo run | 20–60 min |
| **T — Threshold** | Improve endurance | Same as above | Cruise intervals | Repeats up to 15 min each with rest equal to 1/5 of run time; total up to the lesser of 10% of weekly mileage and 60 min |
| **I — Intervals** | Stress aerobic power (VO₂max) | 95–100% VO₂max; 98–100% HRmax | VO₂max intervals | Repeats up to 5 min each with equal or shorter jog recoveries; total up to the lesser of 10 km and 8% of weekly mileage |
| **I — Intervals** | Stress the aerobic system at race pace | Race pace | Race-pace intervals | Repeats up to 1/4 of race distance with equal or shorter rests; total up to the lesser of 10 km and 2–3 × race distance |
| **R — Repetitions** | Improve speed and economy | Mile race pace | Pace reps and strides | Repeats up to 2 min each with full recovery; total up to the lesser of 5 miles and 5% of weekly mileage |
| **R — Repetitions** | Improve speed and economy | Fast and controlled—race pace or faster | Speed reps and fast strides | Repeats up to 1 min each with full recovery; total up to 2,000 m |

> Heart rate is not useful for the R zone because these short efforts do not allow heart rate to reliably reflect the intensity.
